# dear-data-project

About My Project: Tangled Orders

My project is inspired by Dear Data. I am collecting data about Chinese takeaway orders to explore how people order and pay for their food.

My Topic:

My chosen topic is Chinese takeaway orders. I want to find out more about people's ordering habits, including whether they choose delivery or collection, how they place their orders, which payment methods they use and how many items they order.

Data Collection:

I am recording information about Chinese takeaway orders in a spreadsheet and saving the data as a CSV file.
The information I am collecting includes:

Order type: Delivery or collection.
Payment method: For example, cash or card.
Ordering method: For example, ordering directly online, phone call, through Uber Eats or through another platform.
Number of items: How many items are included in each order.

Files:

README.md — information about my project.
data collection.csv — my collected data in CSV format.
dear data collection.xlsx - my collected data in spreadsheet

Progress:

I have started collecting my data in spreadsheet and started to uploaded data as CSV file to GitHub.
